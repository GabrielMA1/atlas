Privacy Policy

How information and local preferences are handled on gmacovei.com.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

What this site collects

gmacovei.com is a personal professional website. It does not provide a personal inquiry form, account registration, checkout, or client portal. If you contact Gabriel by email or through LinkedIn, the information you provide is handled through those services and their own systems.

Local theme preference

The site stores your light or dark appearance preference in your browser using local storage. The preference stays on your device and is used only to display the site in your selected theme.

Analytics, cookies, and advertising

No analytics service is currently implemented on gmacovei.com. The static site does not intentionally set advertising cookies. If analytics or other tracking tools are introduced later, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

External links

This website links to RielArt, the RielArt Client Portal, LinkedIn, email, and article pages hosted by RielArt. Those websites and services have their own privacy practices. Following an external link leaves gmacovei.com.

Privacy questions

For a privacy question about this personal website, email hello@gmacovei.com.
