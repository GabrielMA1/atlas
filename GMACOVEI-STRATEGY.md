# GMACOVEI Personal Website Strategy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

## Current role of the site

gmacovei.com is Gabriel Macovei's personal professional website. It should help a visitor understand who Gabriel is, the areas he works across, how he approaches digital and technical work, selected businesses and products he has helped shape, his writing, and the correct way to connect.

It is not a second agency website, a pricing surface, a service catalogue, or a client workspace.

## Problems with the current positioning

The audited homepage presents Gabriel primarily as a "Digital Strategist & AI Expert" and leads with separate Digital Presence and AI & Automation offers. That structure makes the personal site read like a commercial service page, duplicates work that belongs on RielArt, and overweights AI relative to Gabriel's broader professional profile.

The current page also:

- Uses generic industry and outcome grids without public evidence.
- Repeats broad agency-style claims.
- Describes RielArt through an outdated "Brand · Web · AI systems" model.
- Says RielArt is where client projects live, even though client activity belongs in the Client Portal.
- Uses a service FAQ that repeats information available elsewhere.
- Routes hero actions toward email and LinkedIn instead of first explaining Gabriel's work.
- Carries multiple inherited visual systems and unused component styles.

## New role of gmacovei.com

The site will become a polished, one-page personal profile with a calm editorial hierarchy. It will:

- Establish Gabriel's balanced professional positioning.
- Explain his professional focus without presenting public packages.
- Show RielArt and the RielArt Client Portal as accurate, labelled work.
- Consolidate biography, working principles, and skills.
- Publish a small, current selection of writing links.
- Route business inquiries, professional connections, and direct email correctly.

## Relationship between the three properties

### gmacovei.com

The personal professional profile. It communicates identity, focus, approach, selected work, writing, and professional contact routes.

### rielart.com

The commercial business destination for brand and website work, focused Google or Meta advertising, current scope, pricing, process, and project inquiries.

The personal site will not reproduce package cards, prices, service detail, checkout, or the inquiry form.

### portal.rielart.com

The secure workspace for approved RielArt clients. Live browser inspection on July 28, 2026 confirmed a deployed passwordless sign-in screen restricted to approved portal emails. The personal site will describe only project progress, milestones, updates, and requests. It will not advertise billing, file management, analytics, or integrations.

## Approved personal positioning

- Eyebrow: **Digital Presence · Advertising Management · Practical Systems**
- Headline: **I turn complex digital work into clear, usable systems.**
- Supporting copy: **I'm Gabriel Macovei, a Toronto-based digital professional working across digital presence, advertising management, business technology, automation, and practical systems.**
- Job title for structured data: **Digital Professional**

Business IT, cloud tools, integrations, automation, and practical AI remain accurate supporting capabilities beneath Practical Systems. Advertising Management refers specifically to focused Google Search or Meta advertising and does not imply every platform, unlimited campaigns, guaranteed results, or large creative-production services.

These are professional focus areas, not public packages. Commercial brand, website, and advertising inquiries continue to route to RielArt.

## Homepage architecture

1. Personal hero
2. Professional focus
3. Selected work and products
4. About and working approach
5. RielArt relationship
6. Writing
7. Contact routing
8. Footer

## Sections removed

- Package-led "What I Do" offers
- Generic industries and use cases
- Generic selected outcomes
- Service-style FAQ
- Repeated RielArt proof points
- Three-way hero contact button row

## Sections consolidated

- About, skills, industries, and outcomes become one personal About and working approach section.
- RielArt references become one selected-work item and one concise relationship section with distinct purposes.
- Contact methods become a single routing section with three aligned options.

## Sections added

- Three-area professional focus
- Selected work and products
- Four working principles
- Explicit cross-property routing

## Contact-routing strategy

- Brand, website, and online advertising inquiries go to `https://rielart.com/contact/#project-inquiry`.
- Professional conversations and networking go to LinkedIn.
- Direct professional messages use `mailto:hello@gmacovei.com`.
- No contact form, scheduling embed, or duplicate RielArt qualification flow will be added.

## Visual strategy

The design will preserve the portrait, GMACOVEI logo, navy and blue palette, theme persistence, and responsive behavior while replacing the layered glass-heavy treatment with:

- Strong editorial typography
- A portrait-led first viewport
- Calm light and dark surfaces
- Clear spacing and restrained borders
- Selective, aligned card groups
- Subtle reveal motion with reduced-motion support
- No pointer-following glow

## Truth and disclosure rules

The site will make no client, employer, team, award, certification, revenue, result, project-count, or experience-duration claims. RielArt and its portal will be identified by type. No third portfolio item will be invented.

## Cross-property verification

Live browser checks on July 28, 2026 confirmed that RielArt presents Brand & Website Launch and Focused Ads Management as its two primary commercial services, the project-inquiry anchor exists, and the Client Portal remains restricted to approved client emails. The personal-site routing and copy therefore match the current live cross-property model.
